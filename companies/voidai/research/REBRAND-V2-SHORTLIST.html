The Hybrid Option

These three directions don't have to be mutually exclusive. The strongest approach may be a tiered brand architecture:

TierBrandAudienceUsage
Consumer Productvoid.Retail depositorsLanding page, app, social media
Institutional ProductVoid CapitalFunds, treasuriesInstitutional landing page, whitepapers
Corporate / DevVoid LabsDevelopers, partnersGitHub, docs, technical blog, corporate

This mirrors what Aave (App / Pro / Institutional) and Coinbase (Coinbase / Advanced / Prime) do. One brand family, three faces.
