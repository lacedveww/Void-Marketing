# 1. AAVE

## 1.1 Visual Identity

### Primary Logo Design
- **Core Mark:** "The Ghost Logomark" (nicknamed "Ronnie the Ghost") - a friendly, minimalist ghost silhouette that serves as Aave's ubiquitous global icon
- **Three configurations:** Logomark (ghost standalone), Wordmark (ghost + "Aave" text), Full Logo (combined)
- **Shape/Style:** Organic, rounded ghost form with subtle eyes. The 2024 rebrand refined the eyes to be subtler/more refined for a professional feel
- **Extensions:** 11 logo extensions, circular/square token avatars, 6 Ronnie character variations for community use
- **Philosophy:** The ghost comes from "Aave" meaning "ghost" in Finnish. The mascot evolved from a prominent, playful character to a more subtle presence in the 2024 rebrand - appearing in illustrations rather than dominating the primary logo

### Color Palette

**Legacy/Classic Brand Colors:**
- Aave Purple: `#B6509E`
- Aave Teal/Blue: `#2EBAC6`

**2024+ Rebrand Extended Palette (24-color system):**

| Family | Colors (Hex) |
|--------|-------------|
| Purple | `#9896FF`, `#BDBBFF`, `#E2E0FF` |
| Teal/Green | `#00827B`, `#39BEB7`, `#83EEE8` |
| Orange/Gold | `#FFB400`, `#FFD400`, `#FFF7A5` |
| Blue | `#008AFF`, `#00AEFF`, `#96E9FF`, `#00498B`, `#5589C5`, `#A7D4FF` |
| Red | `#FF3200`, `#FF8130`, `#FFC38B` |
| Cyan | `#39D1F9`, `#A7E9FD`, `#D9F7FF` |
| Neutral | `#211D1D` (dark), `#847E7E` (mid), `#E6E6E6` (light) |

**Primary Scales:** Gray and Purple form the fundamental brand scheme for all critical visual communications.

**Strategic Shift:** Moved from dark noir aesthetic (deep purples, blues, black backgrounds) to white backgrounds with bright pastels (blues, oranges, purples, teals). This was a deliberate move from "exclusive crypto-native protocol" to "approachable financial infrastructure."

### Typography
- **Primary Brand Typeface:** FT Regola Neue (by Formula Type) - a geo-grotesque that merges geometric and grotesque elements
- **Web/App Primary:** "Aave Repro" (variable weight 400-900) - proprietary
- **Monospace:** "Aave Repro Mono" (variable weight 400-900)
- **App Interface:** Inter (the popular open-source UI font)
- **Style:** Sans-serif, clean, modern. Not overly technical, not overly playful - balanced
- **Feel:** The typography prioritizes readability and hierarchy over personality. Whitespace is used deliberately to create breathing room

### Overall Visual Aesthetic
- **Pre-2024:** Dark mode dominant, high information density, rounded sans-serif, hexagonal security patterns, experimental crypto-native aesthetic
- **Post-2024:** Light mode default, generous whitespace, colorful circular motifs representing interconnection and flow, infographic-style concept visualization, mobile-first optimization
- **Design Language:** Circular, modular design system enabling scalability across products and chains

### Dark Mode vs Light Mode
- Supports both via toggle in the application interface
- **Light Mode:** Fetches all data from RPC (the default/modern approach)
- **Normal/Dark Mode:** Uses subgraphs to reduce RPC load
- The rebrand strongly favors light mode for marketing/brand materials, signaling accessibility and trust (dark mode was associated with the "mysterious" crypto era)

## 1.5 Key Rebrand Lessons (ETHLend to Aave)

### The ETHLend to Aave Transformation (2018-2020)
- **Problem:** ETHLend's peer-to-peer model was too slow, low liquidity, losing momentum
- **Solution:** Complete product pivot (peer-to-peer to peer-to-contract/pooled liquidity) necessitated a brand pivot
- **Name Change:** ETHLend (functional, Ethereum-specific) to Aave (abstract, memorable, Finnish for "ghost")
- **Key Lesson:** When your product model fundamentally changes, your brand must change too. A rebrand should signal genuine transformation, not just cosmetics

### The 2024 Visual Rebrand
- **From:** Dark, technical, crypto-native aesthetic built for DeFi insiders
- **To:** Light, accessible, playful minimalism aimed at mainstream financial users
- **Why:** Increased regulatory clarity + push toward mainstream DeFi adoption
- **Trade-offs acknowledged:** Risk of losing technical credibility with institutional users, potential generic fintech aesthetic, reduced distinctiveness

### Corporate Structure Evolution
- Aave Companies rebranded to Avara (2023) - separating the corporate entity from the protocol brand
- Avara encompasses Aave protocol, Lens Protocol (social), GHO stablecoin, Family wallet
- **Lesson:** Separating the corporate entity from the protocol allows both to evolve independently

# 2. COINBASE

## 2.1 Visual Identity

### Primary Logo Design
- **Core Mark:** A geometric icon that functions as both a "window into the digital asset space" and a negative-space "C" letterform/monogram
- **Style:** Clean, geometric, modern. Straight lines with precise proportions
- **Evolution:** The 2022 rebrand (by Moniker SF) introduced the geometric icon and custom font - the biggest visual shift in the brand's history
- **System:** Coinbase, Coinbase Wallet, Coinbase Commerce, and Base each have distinct marks but share the same geometric visual language and blue palette

### Color Palette

**Primary Colors:**
| Color | Hex | Usage |
|-------|-----|-------|
| Blue Ribbon | `#0052FF` | Primary brand color, CTAs, key actions, trust signal |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Light mode backgrounds, clarity, simplicity |
| Woodsmoke | `#0A0B0D` | Dark mode backgrounds, text, sophistication |

**Additional Interface Colors:**
| Color | Hex | Usage |
|-------|-----|-------|
| Navy Dark | `#011082` | Secondary dark blue |
| Bright Blue | `#344AFB` | Interactive elements |
| Gray Blue | `#546084` | Secondary text/UI elements |

**Color Strategy:**
- The signature blue has been consistent since 2013 - one of the longest-running brand elements
- Blue chosen for trust, security, and technological reliability associations
- Intentionally warmer and more retail-friendly than competitors (Binance's yellow, Kraken's purple)
- Three-color system works seamlessly across light and dark modes
- Blue (`#0052FF`) remains constant across both modes as the anchor

### Typography
- **Primary Typeface:** Coinbase Sans (custom, designed by Moniker SF)
- **Family:** 36 styles, 29,000 glyphs across 5 variants: Optical, Display, Text, Micro, and Mono
- **Design Inspiration:** Blue-collar modernist typefaces (Mercator, Neuzeit S, Folio) - functional durability and timeless quirks from traditional financial documents
- **Character:** Geometric and clean, sits between a neutral grotesque and a more characterful sans-serif. Even stroke weight with slightly rounded terminals that soften the feel without going casual
- **Intent:** Challenge contemporary banking's legacy while maintaining financial-grade trustworthiness. "Typographically executed in a striking and stark Swiss-like fashion" that "feels both self-aware and sincere"
- **Purpose:** Works from dense information-heavy UI (Micro/Mono variants) to expressive marketing applications (Display/Optical variants)

### Overall Visual Aesthetic
- Clean, minimalist, professional
- Generous whitespace with functional organization
- Swiss-inspired graphic design sensibility
- Confidence without arrogance - the brand feels like a trusted institution, not a flashy startup
- Bold typography as a primary design element
- Consistent spacing and grid-based layouts

### Dark Mode vs Light Mode
- Both fully supported across app and web
- Light mode: White (#FFFFFF) dominant with blue (#0052FF) accents
- Dark mode: Woodsmoke (#0A0B0D) base with blue (#0052FF) constant, white for text
- Both modes maintain the same visual hierarchy and brand recognition
- Marketing materials increasingly use light mode for accessibility and trust signals

# 3. COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS & KEY LESSONS FOR A DeFi REBRAND

## 3.1 How Aave Evolved (ETHLend > Aave > Avara Ecosystem)

| Phase | Brand | Identity | Audience |
|-------|-------|----------|----------|
| 2017 | ETHLend | Functional name, P2P lending | Crypto early adopters |
| 2020 | Aave V1/V2 | Ghost mascot, dark mode, crypto-native | DeFi power users |
| 2024 | Aave (rebrand) | Light mode, pastels, playful minimalism | Mainstream + institutional |
| 2025-26 | Aave App + Pro + Horizon | Three-tier product branding | Everyone: retail, pro, institutional |

**Key Lesson:** Each rebrand accompanied a fundamental product/market expansion. The brand always followed the product strategy, not the reverse.

## 3.2 How Coinbase Evolved (Exchange > Financial Platform)

| Phase | Positioning | Identity Signal |
|-------|------------|----------------|
| 2012 | "Buy Bitcoin" | Simple, functional |
| 2018 | "Crypto exchange" | Professional, compliant |
| 2022 | "Crypto platform" | Custom type, geometric icon, premium |
| 2025-26 | "Financial app / Update the system" | Institutional, activist, comprehensive |

**Key Lesson:** Coinbase's brand evolution was gradual and consistent. The blue never changed. Each evolution added layers of sophistication without discarding core trust signals.

## 3.3 What Makes Crypto Brands Feel "Institutional Grade"

Based on both case studies:

1. **Custom typography** - Both invested heavily in bespoke typefaces. Generic fonts signal generic products. Financial-document-inspired type signals seriousness.
2. **Restrained color palettes** - Coinbase's 3-color system (blue, white, dark) is more institutional than Aave's 24-color palette. Fewer colors = more authority.
3. **Statistics-led trust** - Both lead with numbers: TVL, users, years operational, audit results.
4. **Compliance signals** - SOC 2 certifications, regulatory licenses, public company status, named audit firms.
5. **Light mode for trust** - Both have shifted toward light mode for brand materials. Dark mode reads as "crypto-native"; light mode reads as "financial institution."
6. **Typography hierarchy over color** - Institutional brands use type weight, size, and spacing to create hierarchy rather than relying on colorful UI elements.
7. **Familiar financial language** - "Savings," "deposits," "interest," "protection" - not "yield farming," "liquidity pools," "staking."
8. **Separation of retail and institutional products** - Both have distinct products for different audiences, each with appropriate branding.

## 3.4 Balancing "Innovative/Crypto" with "Safe/Banking"

| Strategy | Innovative Signal | Safe Signal |
|----------|------------------|-------------|
| **Color** | Accent colors, gradients for marketing | Primary blue/neutral for product |
| **Typography** | Display weights for headlines | Text/Mono weights for data |
| **Imagery** | Abstract/generative visuals | Clean product screenshots |
| **Language** | "Update the system," "DeFi innovation" | "Trusted by default," "balance protection" |
| **Logo** | Distinctive mark (ghost, geometric window) | Professional wordmark |
| **Dark/Light** | Dark mode for pro/developer tools | Light mode for consumer/marketing |
| **Content** | Technical blog posts, developer docs | Educational content, tutorials |
| **Social** | Community governance, protocol updates | Regulatory compliance, partnerships |

## 3.5 Actionable Takeaways for a DeFi Lending Platform Rebrand

### Visual Identity
1. Invest in a **custom or semi-custom typeface** - this is the single biggest signal of brand maturity in crypto
2. Keep the primary palette to **3-4 colors max** for institutional feel (primary brand color + white + dark + one accent)
3. Design a **modular logo system** that works as app icon, monogram, and full lockup
4. Default to **light mode** for all marketing and consumer-facing materials
5. Offer dark mode as an option for pro/developer interfaces

### Brand Architecture
6. Create **distinct product tiers** with clear naming: Consumer (savings/simple), Pro (advanced), Institutional (compliance-first)
7. Use **familiar banking language** for consumer products: "savings," "deposits," "interest rate," "protection"
8. Reserve DeFi/crypto terminology for pro and developer audiences

### Trust Building
9. Lead with **statistics and third-party validation**: TVL, years operational, audit results, security certifications
10. Display **partnership logos and audit firm names** prominently
11. Pursue **SOC 2 Type 2** or equivalent compliance certifications
12. Make **security and audits** a dedicated, prominent section of the website

### Marketing
13. **Education-first content** drives higher-quality users (Coinbase's 35% higher retention from Learn)
14. Balance **advocacy/vision messaging** ("update the system") with **product-benefit messaging** ("earn 6.5% on savings")
15. Build **developer relations** as a separate marketing function with its own voice
16. Use governance and community participation as organic marketing channels

### Digital Presence
17. Hero section should show **one clear value proposition + one clear CTA** (not information overload)
18. Present yield data in **clean, simple formats** using monospace numbers
19. Design the **onboarding flow for under 5 minutes** to first value
20. Use **progressive disclosure** - start simple, reveal complexity on demand
